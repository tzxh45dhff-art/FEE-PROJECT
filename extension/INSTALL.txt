HUDDLE — WATCH TOGETHER

The extension keeps Netflix and Prime Video playback in sync with your Huddle
room. Each viewer needs their own account and access to the title.

1. Extract the downloaded ZIP to a folder you can keep on your computer.
2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
3. Choose Load unpacked and select the extracted folder (the one containing
   manifest.json). Keep the folder in place after installation.
4. Open Huddle, sign in, and enter your room. The extension connects from the
   Huddle page.
5. Open the same film or episode on Netflix or Prime Video. Use the Huddle
   panel on the player to start watching together.

For another computer, each person installs the extension and signs into their
own Huddle and streaming accounts. If the extension was already installed,
replace its extracted files and reload it on chrome://extensions.
